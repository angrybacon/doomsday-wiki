Welcome to the Doomsday Wiki!

The Doomsday Wiki is intended to be the most complete and up to date work on how
to cast the card named Doomsday in the Legacy format of _Magic: the Gathering_.

There is actually much more to how to play the deck, but understanding how to
cast the namesake card of the deck is a pretty big piece of the puzzle. This is
specifically not a _primer_ as you often see for other decks in Legacy, because
primers generally become outdated rather quickly and are more about card
choices, decklists, sideboard tables, and so on. The Doomsday Wiki is there to
help people to really understand how the deck works and can win in various
scenarios.

---

We have three main variants represented on here that you can access resources
for from the left-hand sidebar.

**Doomsday** refers to the primary deck variant that is having previously
unprecedented success since the printing of :card[TO]. Legacy Doomsday uses
countermagic like :card[FoW] and discard like :card[Tsz] to play a controlling
game before ending the game. On occasion and unless it leads to an entirely new
Doomsday engine, a new spoiler will shake the meta up but it will usually be
discussed alongside the main variant.

**DDFT** (Doomsday Fetchland Tendrils) is the closest to historical lists.
Although it adopts the current usage of :card[TO], these decks tend to also have
access to secondary _Storm_ plans using cards like :card[BW], :card[EoE] and
:card[ToA]. They also prefer proactive protection like discard over being able
to run reactive permission.

**Entombsday** is a Tin Fins variant with a maindeck plan focusing on
reanimating :card[Griselbrand] using tools such as :card[Shallow Grave] or
:card[Goryo's Vengeance] to then have it attack alongside :card[Em] or looping
the deck to generate infinite mana and cast your entire library an infinite
number of times. The key point to focus on is that it runs a dedicated
transformational sideboard into a variant of Doomsday.
